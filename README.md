# Ferne | Green Buyout Intelligence

**A renewable-focused public-to-private (P2P) leveraged buyout (LBO) assistant.**

[Visit the website](https://fernechatbot.streamlit.app/)

## About the Project

Ferne was developed during our 2025 summer internship at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS). Our aim was to explore how generative AI could support financial research and simplify the process of assessing potential buyout opportunities in the renewable energy sector.

We built a web application that brings together AI-assisted financial document analysis and interactive industry data, making it easier for users to explore information in one place.

## Key Features

- **AI chatbot:** Ask questions about uploaded financial documents and receive context-based responses using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- **Document upload:** Upload financial reports for analysis through the chatbot.
- **Industry peer multiples:** Explore and compare company valuation multiples through interactive charts.
- **User-friendly interface:** Navigate between the homepage, chatbot, industry multiples and team pages.

## Development

We developed Ferne over two weeks as a team of three, with two members working on technical development and one focusing on the financial domain.

During the first week, we focused on the initial design and development of the application. In the second week, we refined the frontend, added interactive financial charts in place of static images, integrated the frontend and backend, and worked on cloud deployment.

The project gave us practical experience in developing an AI-powered prototype, collaborating across technical and non-technical roles, and overcoming integration and deployment challenges.
